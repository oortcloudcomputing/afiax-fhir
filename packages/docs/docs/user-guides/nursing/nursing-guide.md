# Nursing guide

This page brings together everything a nurse does in Afiax, in the order you'll usually do it during a patient's visit. Each step links to the full guide.

**Who this is for:** nurses, triage staff and midwives.

## Before your first shift

1. [Sign in and set up two-step verification](../front-desk/sign-in.md).
2. Ask your facility administrator to make sure you've been added as a practitioner with your NCK registration checked. See [Add a practitioner](../admin/add-a-practitioner.md).

## During a patient's visit

| Step | What you do | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Find the patient** | Search by ID number or name | [Find an existing patient](../front-desk/find-a-patient.md) |
| **2. Open the visit** | Start the visit if front desk hasn't already, and set the visit type, for example **Antenatal Visit** or **Child Welfare Clinic** | [Start a visit](../clinician/start-a-visit.md) |
| **3. Triage** | Record blood pressure, temperature, pulse, breathing rate, oxygen saturation, weight, height and MUAC | [Record vital signs](../clinician/record-vital-signs.md) |
| **4. Check for alerts and allergies** | Read the clinical summary. Ask the patient about allergies and record any new ones. | [Clinical summary and allergies](../clinician/clinical-summary.md) |
| **5. Family history** | Record illnesses in the family, for example at a first ANC visit | [Record family history](../clinician/family-history.md) |
| **6. Follow up orders** | See whether lab tests and prescriptions have been done | [Place an order and follow it up](../clinician/place-an-order.md#follow-up-an-order) |

## Things to watch for

- **Red alerts.** A dangerous reading raises an alert on the patient's record automatically. It doesn't replace escalation: tell the clinician straight away, following your facility's procedure.
- **Children.** For patients under 18, BMI isn't categorised. Use the **Growth** tab of the [clinical summary](../clinician/clinical-summary.md#growth) instead, so record weight and height at every child visit.
- **Allergies.** Record every allergy the patient mentions, even if you're not sure it's real. Choose **Unable to assess** for the criticality. Prescriptions are checked against this list, so a missing allergy can't protect the patient.

## If you prescribe or order

Nurses who are authorised to order tests or prescribe, for example in nurse-led clinics, follow the same guides as clinicians:

- [Place an order](../clinician/place-an-order.md)
- [Prescribe a medicine](../clinician/prescribe-a-medicine.md)

## Not available yet

These nursing tasks don't have a screen in Afiax yet, so keep using your current paper or ward process for them:

- Recording medicine administration (a medication administration record, or MAR)
- Nursing notes and care plans
- Ward admission, bed management and fluid balance
