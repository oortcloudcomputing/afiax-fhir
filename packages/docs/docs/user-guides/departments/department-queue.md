# Work your department queue

When a clinician places an order, it goes straight into the queue of the department that does the work. This guide shows department staff how to pick up orders, work on them, and mark them done, so the clinician can see the progress.

**Who this is for:** pharmacy, laboratory, radiology, physiotherapy, nutrition, social work and counselling staff.

**How long it takes:** a few seconds per order.

## How an order moves through the queue

| Status | What it means | Who moves it on |
|---|---|---|
| **requested** | The clinician has placed the order. Nobody has picked it up yet. | You click **Accept** |
| **in progress** | Your department is working on it | You click **Complete**, or **Dispense** in the pharmacy |
| **Completed** or **Dispensed** | Done. It leaves your queue. | Nobody: it's finished |

The clinician sees each change straight away in the visit's **Order history**.

## Before you start

You need an Afiax account. In the left menu you should see **Department queues**. If you don't, ask your facility administrator.

## Step 1: Open your queue

1. In the left menu, click **Department queues**. The pharmacy queue opens first.
2. At the top of the page, click your department: **Pharmacy**, **Laboratory**, **Radiology**, **Physiotherapy**, **Nutrition**, **Social work** or **Counselling**.

:::tip
Bookmark your department's queue so it opens straight away next time.
:::

You'll see a table of every open order for your department, oldest first, so the patient who has waited longest is at the top. If there are none, the page says *No open orders for*, followed by your department's name.

Each row shows:

- **Ordered:** when the clinician placed the order.
- **Patient:** click the name to open the patient's record.
- **Order:** what was ordered. Click it to see the full order. For medicines, the dosage and quantity are shown underneath in grey.
- **Priority:** a grey **routine** badge, or a red **urgent** or **stat** badge.
- **Status:** where the order has got to.

:::caution
Look for red priority badges first. **stat** means immediately, and comes before everything else, even if it's lower down the list.
:::

## Step 2: Accept an order

When you start on an order, for example when you call the patient in or start preparing a medicine:

1. Find the order in the table.
2. Click **Accept**.

**What you should see:** a green message saying **Order accepted**. The status changes to **in progress**, and you're recorded as the person handling it. This tells your colleagues and the clinician that someone is working on it.

## Step 3: Complete the order

When the work is finished:

- **In the pharmacy:** hand over the medicine, check the dosage with the patient, then click **Dispense**.
- **In every other department:** when the test, study or session is done, click **Complete**.

**What you should see:** a green message saying **Dispensed** or **Completed**, and the order leaves your queue.

You can click **Complete** or **Dispense** without clicking **Accept** first, for example if you finish a quick order straight away.

:::caution
There's no confirmation question and no undo on this screen. Check you've got the right patient and order before you click **Complete** or **Dispense**.
:::

## What Dispense records

In the pharmacy, **Dispense** does more than close the order. It records that the medicine was handed over: which medicine, the quantity, the dosage instructions, the time, and your name. The dispensing then shows in the patient's **Medication history**.

## If something goes wrong

### A new order doesn't appear

The queue doesn't refresh by itself. Refresh the browser page (press **F5**) to see new orders.

### I clicked Complete or Dispense by mistake

Tell your supervisor or facility administrator straight away, so the record can be corrected. Don't try to place the order again yourself.

### The order is unclear

Click the patient's name to read their record, or contact the clinician who placed the order. Don't guess.

## Good to know

- **Lab and imaging results** aren't entered on this screen. **Complete** only marks the order as done.
- **Several people can work the same queue.** Accepting an order shows the others that it's taken.
