# Invite a staff member

Use this guide to give a new staff member an Afiax account. They receive an email invitation and set their own password.

**Who this is for:** facility administrators.

**How long it takes:** about 2 minutes per person.

## Before you start

You need:

- Administrator access to Afiax. You have it if you can see the **Admin** section in the left menu.
- The staff member's first name, last name and work email address.
- For clinicians: add them as a practitioner first, so their licence is checked. See [Add a practitioner](./add-a-practitioner.md).

## Steps

1. In the left menu, under **Admin**, click **Project**.
2. Click the **Users** tab. You'll see everyone who already has access.
3. Click **Invite New User**. A page headed **Invite new member** opens.
4. **Role:** leave it as **Practitioner** for all staff, clinical or not. **Patient** and **RelatedPerson** are for patient portal access, which isn't covered here.
5. Type their **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email**.
6. **Access Policy:** choose the policy for their job, for example a cashier or clinician policy. The policy decides what they can see and do. If you're not sure which to use, ask Afiax support. Don't leave it empty unless you've been told to.
7. Choose the options:

   | Option | Tick it when |
   |---|---|
   | **Send email** | Always, unless you'll give them the link another way. Ticked by default. |
   | **Admin** | Only for people who will manage users and settings. Most staff should **not** be admins. |
   | **Project scoped** | They should only have access to this facility |
   | **MFA required** | Recommended for everyone: it makes them set up [two-step verification](../front-desk/sign-in.md#set-up-two-step-verification) |

8. Click **Invite**.

**What you should see:** the message **User created** and **Email sent**. Tell the staff member to look for the email, and to follow [Sign in and keep your account secure](../front-desk/sign-in.md).

## If something goes wrong

### "User created, email couldn't be sent"

The account exists but the email didn't go out. Contact Afiax support to resend it. Don't invite the person again.

### The staff member never receives the email

Ask them to check their spam folder. If it isn't there, check the email address on the **Users** tab, then contact Afiax support.

## When someone leaves

Remove their access on the day they leave. On the **Users** tab, open the person and remove their membership, or ask Afiax support to do it. Never share one person's account with another.
