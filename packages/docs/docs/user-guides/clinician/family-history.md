# Record family history

Use this guide to record illnesses in the patient's family, such as a mother with diabetes or a brother with sickle cell disease. Family history stays on the patient's record, so you only need to record each item once.

**Who this is for:** clinicians and nurses.

**How long it takes:** under a minute per item.

## Before you start

You need a visit open for the patient. See [Start a visit](./start-a-visit.md). Family history can be added to any visit, even one that has finished.

## Steps

1. Open the patient's visit.
2. In the **Clinical orders** panel, click the **Family history** tab.
3. Read the table at the top. It lists everything already recorded for this patient, from any visit, so you don't record the same thing twice. If there's nothing yet, it says *No family history recorded.*
4. In **Relative**, choose who had the condition, for example **Mother**.
5. Click **Condition (ICD-11)**, type at least 2 letters of the condition, for example `diab`, and choose the right ICD-11 diagnosis from the list.
6. If it helps, add a **Note**, for example *diagnosed aged 45*.
7. Click **Add family history**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: Family history tab with a new item filled in */}

**What you should see:** the new item appears in the table at the top, and the form empties.

## If something goes wrong

### Add family history is greyed out

Choose both a **Relative** and a **Condition (ICD-11)** from the list. Typing the condition isn't enough: you must click one of the matches.

### "No KNHTS match"

Try a shorter word or another name for the condition. All diagnoses use ICD-11 codes from the national terminology service (KNHTS).
