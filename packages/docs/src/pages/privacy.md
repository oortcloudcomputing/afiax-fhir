# Privacy Policy

This page summarises how **Oortcloud Computing Limited, trading as Afiax Connected Healthcare**, handles personal data in Afiax Clinical Suite. The binding text is the full Afiax Privacy Policy (reference AFIAX-PP-001), issued under the Data Protection Act 2019 s.25, the Digital Health Act 2023 s.24 and the Digital Health Regulations. To request the full policy, email [privacy@afiax.africa](mailto:privacy@afiax.africa).

## Who we are

- **Company:** Oortcloud Computing Limited, trading as Afiax Connected Healthcare. P.O. Box 22888, Nairobi, Kenya.
- **Registration:** Registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as a data controller and data processor.
- **Our role:** When a health facility uses Afiax Clinical Suite, the facility is normally the data controller for its patients, and we process data on its behalf.
- **Privacy contact:** [privacy@afiax.africa](mailto:privacy@afiax.africa).
- **Data Protection Officer:** [dpo@afiax.africa](mailto:dpo@afiax.africa).

## What we collect

- **Patients:** identity details (name, date of birth, sex, National ID, SHA Member ID, Client Registry ID, MRN), contact details and next of kin. Also the health information needed for care: diagnoses (ICD-11), vital signs, medications, allergies, laboratory results, imaging, procedures, and SHA claim and co-pay records.
- **Health workers:** name, KMPDC or NCK licence number, Health Worker Registry ID, role, facility, sign-in records and an audit trail of clinical actions.

We collect only what is needed to provide and record care. We do not collect data for advertising or marketing.

## Why we use it

- To deliver and record clinical care, including clinical decision support alerts. These are advisory only: a licensed clinician always makes the decision.
- To verify patient identity with the DHA Client Registry, and share a Shared Health Record through the Kenya Health Information Exchange for continuity of care.
- To submit Social Health Authority (SHA) claims and collect co-payments (for example, through M-Pesa).
- To meet public-health reporting duties: aggregate KHIS (DHIS2) reports and IDSR disease notifications. KHIS reports contain counts only, never patient identifiers.
- To keep the system secure, through audit logging and monitoring.

## Who we share it with

We share only the minimum needed, and only with:

- The **Digital Health Agency** (Client Registry and Health Information Exchange).
- The **Social Health Authority**.
- The **Ministry of Health** (aggregate reports and legally required notifications).
- **KMPDC / NCK** (practitioner details only).

Our sub-processors are **Safaricom** (M-Pesa payments) and **Liquid Intelligent Technologies** (data-centre hosting in Kenya, with no access to data content). We never sell or rent personal data.

## Where it is stored

All identifiable health data is stored and processed **only in Kenya**, in two Liquid data centres (Nairobi, plus a second Kenyan site for disaster recovery). Health data does not leave Kenya, except through the authorisation procedure in the Digital Health Regulations when a patient needs care abroad.

## How we protect it

- Personal accounts with mandatory multi-factor authentication.
- Role-based access that limits each user to what their job requires.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Encrypted backups held in Kenya.
- Audit logs of every access and change, kept in write-once storage.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Patient health records and imaging | At least 20 years after the last update |
| Audit logs | 20 years, in write-once storage |
| Other records (claims, recordings, access logs) | As set out in the full policy and the law that applies |

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019 you can:

- ask for a copy of your data
- have inaccurate data corrected
- receive your record in a portable format (FHIR R4)
- object to or restrict some processing
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- ask for erasure. Health records must legally be kept for 20 years, so erasure may not be possible within that period.

To use any of these rights, email [privacy@afiax.africa](mailto:privacy@afiax.africa) or [dpo@afiax.africa](mailto:dpo@afiax.africa). We respond within 30 days, or within 72 hours for corrections.

You can also complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner**: [www.odpc.go.ke](https://www.odpc.go.ke), complaints@odpc.go.ke.

## Cookies

Afiax Clinical Suite uses only strictly necessary cookies: your sign-in session, protection against cross-site request forgery, and the sign-in flow itself. We do not use tracking, advertising or third-party analytics cookies.
