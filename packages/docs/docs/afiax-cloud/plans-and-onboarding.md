# Plans and Onboarding

Afiax is sold as an annual subscription **per facility**, for each product you license, plus a one-time onboarding fee per deployment. For prices, email [info@afiax.africa](mailto:info@afiax.africa).

## Plans

Every plan includes full access to the products you license. Plans differ in facility size and in the support and integration work that comes with them.

| | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | Clinics and health centres with up to 10 clinical staff | Hospitals and multi-specialty clinics with 10–50 clinical staff | County hospitals, large private hospitals and multi-site networks |
| **Products** | Any of Clinical Suite, Afiax AI (add-on to Clinical Suite) and Workspace, or the full bundle | Same | Same |
| **Support** | Afiax Helpdesk, with service levels as in the [support SLA](./support-and-sla) | Same | Adds dedicated support and contracted SLA guarantees |
| **Integrations** | Standard Kenyan integrations (SHA, HIE, KHIS, M-Pesa) | Same | Adds an allowance for custom integrations |

Subscriptions are invoiced annually in advance, in USD. Kenyan customers can pay by M-Pesa or local bank transfer.

## What onboarding includes

Onboarding is done by Oortcloud's implementation team. It typically follows these steps:

1. **Project set-up.** We create your organisation's Afiax project and enable the Kenya country pack.
2. **Facility verification.** We verify your facility against the national facility registry (MFL code) through the DHA Health Information Exchange, and record your SHA details.
3. **Users and roles.** We create accounts for your staff in Afiax IAM. Every user has their own login with multi-factor authentication. We verify practitioners' KMPDC, NCK or PPB registrations, and assign roles so each person sees only what their job needs.
4. **Configuration.** We set up your departments, services, price lists, payment methods (M-Pesa, Afiax Pay, cash, card, insurers) and reporting.
5. **Data migration (optional).** We move patient records from your previous system into Afiax. See [Migration](/docs/migration).
6. **Devices and on-site connections (if needed).** For laboratory analysers or other on-site devices, we install the [Afiax Facility Agent](/docs/agent) on a computer at your facility.
7. **Training.** We train your Training Champions, who then support their colleagues day to day. See the user training materials provided during onboarding.
8. **Go-live.** We agree a go-live date, stay on hand during the first days, and hand over to the Afiax Helpdesk.

## What we need from you

- A named project lead and at least one Training Champion per facility.
- Facility details: MFL code, SHA contract details, departments and services.
- A staff list with roles and professional registration numbers.
- Reliable internet and up-to-date browsers on the computers that will use Afiax. Your network and devices remain your responsibility (see [Operations and responsibilities](./operations-and-updates#who-does-what)).
