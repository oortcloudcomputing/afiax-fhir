# Report a notifiable disease (IHR)

Some diseases must be reported to the World Health Organization (WHO) under the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005). Afiax recognises these diseases from the diagnosis, walks you through the WHO assessment, prepares the report, and records each step of the notification chain.

**Who this is for:** clinicians, and the facility's disease surveillance focal person.

**How long it takes:** about 5 minutes for the assessment.

:::caution
This is not a replacement for a phone call. For any suspected outbreak or always-notifiable disease, contact your County Disease Surveillance Coordinator straight away, following the national IDSR guidelines. Use Afiax to record and track the notification.
:::

## Before you start

You need:

- The patient's diagnosis recorded with its ICD-11 code. The IHR section appears on the diagnosis record.
- The facts of the case: exposure and travel history, control measures taken, the number of cases and the number of deaths.

## Step 1: Open the diagnosis

1. Open the patient's diagnosis record.
2. Scroll to the section headed **IHR (2005) International Notification**.

A badge next to the heading tells you what to do:

| Badge | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Red **IHR: always notifiable** | A single case must be notified to WHO within 24 hours, for example smallpox or a new influenza subtype | Go to [Step 2](#step-2-run-the-assessment). The assessment questions are skipped. |
| Orange **IHR: assessment required** | The disease is listed in IHR Annex 2, for example cholera or yellow fever. It must be reported if at least 2 of 4 criteria apply. | Go to [Step 2](#step-2-run-the-assessment) and answer the four questions. |
| Grey **Not an IHR Annex 2 disease** | The disease isn't on the IHR list | Usually nothing. If the event is still unusual or serious, click **Assess as an event of potential international concern** and carry on with step 2. |

{/* SCREENSHOT: IHR section with the orange "assessment required" badge */}

## Step 2: Run the assessment

If the panel shows **Assessment due by**, followed by a date and time, finish the assessment before then.

1. For an **assessment required** disease, answer each question **Yes** or **No**:
   - Is the public health impact of the event serious?
   - Is the event unusual or unexpected?
   - Is there a significant risk of international spread?
   - Is there a significant risk of international travel or trade restrictions?
2. In **Exposure and travel history**, describe where the patient has been and who they've been in contact with.
3. In **Control measures taken**, describe what has been done, for example *patient isolated; contacts listed*.
4. Enter the **Cases in this event** and the number of **Deaths**.
5. Click **Run IHR assessment**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: completed assessment form */}

### Reading the result

- **Green: "Not notifiable to WHO".** The event doesn't need to be reported to WHO. The panel shows **Assessed: not notifiable to WHO** with the reasoning. Report it locally through IDSR as usual. If the situation changes, click **Reassess**.
- **Red: "Notifiable to WHO".** Afiax has generated the IHR report and started the notification chain. The panel shows the deadline, usually 24 hours. Go to step 3.

## Step 3: Record each notification step

The notification goes up a chain, and the panel shows each step:

1. County Director of Health notified
2. National IHR Focal Point (MoH Disease Surveillance and Response Unit) notified
3. WHO notified by the National IHR Focal Point

For each step, once it has happened:

1. In the box labelled **Record:** followed by the step, type who was contacted, how, and any reference number. For example: `Dr A. Mwangi, CDH Kisumu, by phone 14:20, ref IDSR/2026/118`.
2. Click **Record step**.

**What you should see:** a green message saying **Escalation step recorded**. The step is ticked off, and your note appears below it with the time.

To send the report to the County, click **Download report**. This saves it as a file you can attach to an email. The report's contents are also shown on the screen.

## If something goes wrong

### I can't find the IHR section

The section only appears on a diagnosis record, not on the patient's main record. Check that you've opened the diagnosis.

### A red error box appears

Note the message and the time, then contact your facility administrator or Afiax support. Don't wait for the system: notify the County by phone in the meantime.

## Good to know

- Every assessment and every step is recorded with your name and the time, so the notification can be audited.
- The 24-hour deadline is set by IHR Article 6.
